## Evidence labels

- **Verified** — Verified means the public page or HTTP response was inspected. Partial means an official indexed extract was available but direct retrieval was limited. Unknown is not zero or absence.
- **Limits** — No authenticated GSC/GA4 exports, backlink dataset, live Google ranks, keyword volume, rendered-mobile audit or Core Web Vitals measured. No website changes deployed.
- **Historical baseline** — User brief mixes monthly and 90-day periods and repeats SignalAF totals as all-domain totals. Do not sum it into a verified baseline. Impressions/clicks are search visibility, not site sessions.
