# SEO Audit — Action Plan

## P1 — Unify the paid assessment promise

**Impact:** High
**Effort:** 2–4 hours
**Estimated results:** Same day for clarity; 2–6 weeks for search changes
**Confidence:** Verified live; recommended primary URL is an editorial choice

**Affected pages:** mos2es.org/baseline-assessment; /pilot; /commercial-offer; /methodology

**Evidence:** The baseline page offers CAUSATION; methodology says ASSOCIATION never CAUSATION. The three offer pages also describe different delivery schedules.

**Recommended change:** Use one agreed scope, schedule, price basis and evidence vocabulary. Remove the automatic causation label. Make /baseline-assessment the principal offer page after merging the current commercial terms. Preserve a distinct configurator only if it serves a separate function.

**Acceptance check:** All offer CTAs resolve to the agreed offer; no contradictory claims remain.

**Impact:** High

**Impact:** High
**Effort:** 2–3 hours

**Confidence:** Measurement recommendation; not a request to repeat business context
